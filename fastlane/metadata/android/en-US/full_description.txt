A fast, simple note taker that keeps everything on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Features:
- The list shows only note names, so it stays tidy
- Select words and tap "Use as name" to choose a note's list name
- 12 soft colors
- Pin notes to a block at the top; edited notes move up
- Archive and Recycle with a one-tap Undo
- Lock a note so it cannot be changed or deleted by accident
- Search through all your notes
- Works fully offline
- Export and import backups as a simple .json file

Your notes are stored only on your device. Export a backup now and then.
